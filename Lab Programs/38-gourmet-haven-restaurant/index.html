Our Heritage

A Tradition of Gastronomic Distinction

Founded in 2017 in Chennai's historic cultural quarter, Gourmet Haven was conceived as a sanctuary where international culinary rigor meets South Indian terroir.

Chef Antoine Moreau

Executive Chef & Co-Founder

Trained across Michelin-starred kitchens in Lyon and Paris, Chef Moreau brings 18 years of classical French precision, sauces, and fermentation technique.

Chef Meera Raman

Chef de Cuisine

A native of Madurai with a diploma from Le Cordon Bleu London, Meera synthesizes heirloom Tamil aromatics, tamarind reductions, and modern sous-vide textures.
