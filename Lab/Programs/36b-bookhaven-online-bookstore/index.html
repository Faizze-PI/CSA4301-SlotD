Frequently Asked Questions & Store Policies

How fast is delivery within India?
Standard shipping takes 2-4 business days across metropolitan areas and 4-6 business days for rural locations. Express 24-hour delivery is available for orders placed before 1:00 PM IST.
What is BookHaven's return and replacement policy?
We accept hassle-free returns within 14 days of delivery if the book arrives damaged, has missing pages, or differs from your order. Reverse pick-up is arranged at zero customer cost.
Can educational institutions order in bulk with tax invoices?
Yes! College libraries and research institutions are eligible for up to 25% institutional discounts with standard GST tax invoices.
Privacy Policy & Data Protection
BookHaven does not sell or share customer personal information with third-party advertisers. All checkout transactions are secured using 256-bit SSL encryption.
