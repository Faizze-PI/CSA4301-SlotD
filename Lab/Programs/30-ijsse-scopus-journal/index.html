Author Instructions & Preparation Checklist

  • Formatting: Manuscripts must follow the standard double-column IEEE/IJSSE format (max 10 pages).
  • Originality & Plagiarism: Articles undergo mandatory Turnitin screening (Strictly < 10% similarity index).
  • References: Reference citations must be numbered in square brackets [1], [2] in APA/IEEE standard.
  • Peer Review Process: Double-blind peer review by at least two independent international experts.

About IJSSE & Publication Ethics

Founded in 2014, the International Journal of Scientific and Social Excellence (IJSSE) provides an international forum for cutting-edge interdisciplinary breakthroughs in computer engineering, data science, bio-informatics, and social economics.

COPE Code of Conduct & Ethics Statement

IJSSE strictly follows the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) core practices. Authors, reviewers, and editors must adhere to zero tolerance on data falsification, duplicate submission, and author gift disputes. Authors retain copyright under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
