Artistic Philosophy & Trajectory

Emily Harris (b. 1988) is an internationally exhibited British painter and composer known for large-scale immersive canvases that marry dynamic gestural brushwork with atmospheric glazing. Her signature series, "Resonances of Solitude", earned critical praise at the Venice Biennale and the Tate Modern.

Working primarily with hand-ground mineral pigments, raw linen, and acoustic field recordings, Emily treats each canvas as a physical score. Her visual pieces are frequently exhibited alongside site-specific binaural soundscapes created in her Cornwall studio.

Notable Career Milestones

  • 2024: Grand Laureate, Venice Contemporary Art Biennale.
  • 2022: Solo Retrospective, Echoes in Ochre, Hayward Gallery, London.
  • 2019: Resident Master Artist, Villa Medici French Academy, Rome.
  • 2015: MFA Distinction in Painting, Royal College of Art.
